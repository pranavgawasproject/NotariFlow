# Privacy Policy for NotaryFlow

**Last Updated: June 16, 2025**

## Introduction

NotaryFlow ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NotaryFlow mobile and web application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you register for NotaryFlow, we collect:
- **Email address** - For account authentication
- **Name** - For your business profile
- **Business information** - Company name, phone, address (optional)

### Usage Data
We automatically collect:
- **Location data** - GPS coordinates when you track mileage trips (stored locally on your device)
- **Device information** - Operating system, browser type
- **App usage** - Features accessed, session duration

### Business Data
Data you create in the app:
- **Client information** - Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses
- **Invoice records** - Amounts, dates, services provided
- **Mileage logs** - Trip distances, purposes, timestamps

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected data for:

1. **Service Provision**
   - Creating and managing your account
   - Storing your business data securely
   - Syncing data across devices

2. **App Functionality**
   - Calculating mileage and fees
   - Generating invoices and reports
   - Providing analytics and insights

3. **Communication**
   - Sending service updates
   - Responding to support requests
   - Notifying about important changes

4. **Improvement**
   - Analyzing usage patterns
   - Fixing bugs and errors
   - Developing new features

## Data Storage and Security

### Where Data is Stored
- **Firebase Cloud Firestore** - Hosted on Google Cloud Platform (US servers)
- **Your device** - Location data cached locally for performance

### Security Measures
- **Encryption** - All data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL)
- **Authentication** - Secure Firebase Authentication
- **Access controls** - You control who sees your data
- **Regular backups** - Automatic cloud backups

### Data Retention
- **Active accounts** - Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted accounts** - Data permanently deleted within 30 days
- **Legal requirements** - May retain data if required by law

## Location Data

### Why We Collect It
- To track mileage for business trips
- To calculate IRS deductions
- To provide accurate distance measurements

### How It's Used
- **Only when you start a trip** - We don't track you constantly
- **Stored on your device** - GPS data stays local
- **Never shared** - Location data is private to you

### Your Control
- **Permission required** - You must enable location access
- **Can be disabled** - Turn off in device settings
- **Manual entry** - Enter mileage without GPS
